Context

An AI system recommends whether insurance claims should receive additional fraud review. Human reviewers determine the final disposition of individual claims.

Available evidence

- Each model recommendation contains a timestamp and model version.
- Human reviewers can accept or override model recommendations.
- Overrides are recorded.
- Recording a rationale for an override is optional.
- The process documentation lists the Fraud Operations team and Automated Decision-Support team as jointly responsible for operation of the process.
- The documentation available to the evaluator contains no rule assigning responsibility for an individual decision when the reviewer and model recommendation agree.
- Reviewers report that unusual cases are commonly discussed with senior colleagues.
- Customers can submit complaints through the organisation's general complaints process.
- The process documentation available to the evaluator does not describe how such complaints are connected to reconsideration of the original assessment.

Evaluation request

Assess whether the available evidence supports attributing accountable decision-making to the described AI-assisted process.
