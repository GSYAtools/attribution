Context

An AI-assisted credit screening system prioritises loan applications for manual review. Human analysts make the final lending decision.

Available evidence

- Validation accuracy: 93.1%.
- Overall false-positive rate: 7.9%.
- Overall false-negative rate: 6.2%.
- Overall calibration error: 0.029.
- The validation dataset contains 51,600 applications.
- Group-specific error rates are not reported in the validation documentation.
- The demographic composition of the validation dataset is not included in the documentation available to the evaluator.
- During the first eight weeks of limited deployment, 14,310 applications were processed.
- Seven complaints concerning lending decisions were registered; none had completed investigation at the date of evaluation.

Evaluation request

Assess whether the available evidence supports attributing fairness to the system for the described deployment context.

---

Evaluation Criteria - Fairness

Assess fairness using evidence relevant to the distribution of system outcomes and errors across groups relevant to the intended deployment context. Consider whether the available evaluation data support meaningful group-level comparison and whether the population represented in the evaluation evidence corresponds adequately to the intended deployment population. Aggregate predictive performance alone should not be treated as sufficient evidence of fairness.
