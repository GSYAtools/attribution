===== USER PROMPT =====

Context

An AI system classifies medical documents into administrative workflow categories. Staff review classifications before downstream processing.

Available evidence

- Standard-condition validation accuracy: 95.2%.
- With 10% randomly removed non-critical fields, accuracy was 93.7%.
- With the predefined OCR degradation transformation, accuracy was 92.1%.
- Across the predefined formatting-variation set, accuracy ranged from 91.6% to 94.8%.
- An out-of-distribution test set contained 1,200 documents from document classes excluded from training.
- Of the 1,200 out-of-distribution samples, 1,127 were flagged by the out-of-distribution mechanism.
- The validation protocol contains three predefined perturbation tests; corresponding accuracies were 92.8%, 90.9%, and 93.4%.
- A separate validation team reproduced the reported tests.

Evaluation request

Assess whether the available evidence supports attributing operational robustness to the system for the described deployment context.

---

Evaluation Criteria - Operational Robustness

Assess operational robustness using evidence concerning system behaviour across conditions relevant to the intended deployment scope. Consider performance beyond standard validation conditions, including variation, perturbation, distributional change, or other conditions that may affect system behaviour. Evidence from nominal operating conditions alone should not be treated as sufficient evidence of robustness across the deployment scope.
