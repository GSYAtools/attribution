===== USER PROMPT =====

Context

An AI system recommends whether insurance claims should receive additional fraud review. Human reviewers determine the final disposition of individual claims.

Available evidence

- Each model recommendation record contains the model version, timestamp, and a reference to the input record.
- The human review record identifies the evidence consulted.
- The final disposition is stored separately from the model recommendation.
- Reviewers enter a free-text rationale when finalising a disposition.
- The documented procedure assigns the final case-level decision to the claims reviewer.
- Disagreements between the model recommendation and the reviewer are routed to an organisational role identified as Senior Claims Reviewer.
- Customers may request reconsideration of an adverse disposition.
- Reconsideration outcomes are retained with the original decision record.
- Model maintenance is assigned to the Model Operations team.

Evaluation request

Assess whether the available evidence supports attributing accountable decision-making to the described AI-assisted process.

---

Evaluation Criteria - Accountable Decision-Making

Assess accountability by considering whether responsibility for relevant decisions can be identified and whether the process preserves sufficient information to reconstruct and review how those decisions were reached. Consider the relationship between automated recommendations and human decisions, the allocation of decision responsibility, the availability of rationale, and the mechanisms through which decisions can be questioned or reconsidered. Technical logging alone should not be treated as sufficient evidence of accountability.
