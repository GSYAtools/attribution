# SC5 Trustworthiness Attribution Pilot

Controlled pilot study for evaluating whether explicit evaluative conditions
affect trustworthiness attribution from identical technical evidence.

## Research context

The experiment supports SC5 — Evaluative Governance — of the doctoral research
programme.

The central distinction investigated is:

Technical Evidence != Trustworthiness Attribution

The study does not assume that trustworthiness attribution has a unique ground
truth. Instead, it investigates whether explicit evaluative conditions affect
the way attribution is grounded, withheld, escalated, and revised.

## Experimental conditions

C0 = Evidence

C1 = Evidence + externally grounded evaluation criteria

C2 = Evidence + externally grounded evaluation criteria
     + Evaluative Governance conditions

The primary experimental contrast for SC5 is:

C2 - C1

## Pilot purpose

The pilot is intended to assess:

- vignette ambiguity;
- prompt compliance;
- ceiling and floor effects;
- discriminative capacity of the experimental manipulation;
- epistemic restraint;
- evidential grounding;
- selective revisability.

Pilot results are not used for confirmatory hypothesis testing.
